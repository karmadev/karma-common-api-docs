This guide explains how to interpret the purchase and line-item data returned by
[`GET /api/v1/purchases`](/api/latest#tag/Purchases), so an accounting, inventory, or
BI integration reads the numbers the same way Karma does. It complements the field
descriptions on the endpoint itself.

## The shape

A **purchase** is one payment. It has a `status`, an `amount` (total, in cents), a
`currency`, timestamps, and a list of **line items**. Each line item is one thing on the
receipt — a product, a tip, or an amount paid toward a split bill.

All money is in **integer minor units** (öre/cents), and all prices are **VAT-inclusive**.

## Which purchases are sales?

Treat a purchase as a finalized sale only when:

```
status === "confirmed"
```

`pending` is in-flight, and `canceled` / `failed` never took payment. Filter server-side
with the `status` query parameter.

## Which line items are products?

Line items have a `type`:

| `type` | Meaning | Include in a product-sales export? |
|--------|---------|-----------------------------------|
| `product` | A sellable catalogue item | **Yes** |
| `tip` | A gratuity line | No |
| `amount` | An abstract amount paid toward a split/partial bill | No |

Discounts are **not** separate line items — they are a field on the product line (see
below).

## Prices, quantity, and VAT

Read these fields per line item:

| Field | What it is |
|-------|-----------|
| `finalAmountCents` | **Authoritative per-unit price**, VAT-inclusive, **after** discount. |
| `totalAmountCents` | Line total = `finalAmountCents × quantity`. **May be null/0 on some legacy purchases** — if you need a guaranteed line total, compute `finalAmountCents × quantity` yourself. |
| `appliedTotalDiscountAmountCents` | Discount on this line, a positive number, **already subtracted** from the amounts above. Gross before discount = `totalAmountCents + appliedTotalDiscountAmountCents`. |
| `quantity` | Whole-unit count. |
| `quantityCents` | `quantity × 100` — divide by 100 for fractional/weight-based items. |
| `vatRateBasisPoints` | VAT rate in basis points; `÷ 100` for the percentage (`1200` → 12%, `2500` → 25%). |
| `vatAmountCents` | VAT **per unit** (already contained in `finalAmountCents`). |
| `totalVatAmountCents` | VAT for the whole line = `vatAmountCents × quantity`. |

> **Don't rely on `pre_discount_value_cents` or `total_with_modifications_cents`.** The
> former no longer exists; the latter is rarely populated. `finalAmountCents` +
> `appliedTotalDiscountAmountCents` are the fields to build on.

## Modifiers and bundles

Modifiers show up two ways:

- **Inline**, in `appliedModifications` — an array like
  `[{ title, priceAdjustmentCents, variantRef? }]`. Discounts and vouchers appear here as
  negative `priceAdjustmentCents`. These entries **do not** carry an article number.
- **As child line items**, when a variant/add-on maps to its own catalogue item. The child
  row points at its parent via `parentLineItemId`.

> ⚠️ **Avoid double-counting.** Because add-ons can appear as separate child lines, summing
> every line's total can over- or under-count a bundle depending on how the product is
> modelled. Reconcile your per-purchase line sum against the purchase `amount` as a check.

## Matching a sale line to your own product catalogue

`articleNo` is a **Karma-internal reference used for fiscal cash-register registration** —
despite the name it is **not** your SKU/PLU/article number, and it will not match your
catalogue.

To link a sale line to your own product, use **`originItemId`** — the catalogue item the
line was sold from. Join `originItemId` to the item, where your own external article number
lives (populated from your inventory/POS setup). That join, not `articleNo`, is the stable
key to your catalogue.

## Refunds

Refunds are represented against the purchase. Refund amounts are stored as **positive**
values (the magnitude refunded) — negate them for a ledger. A refund is valid once it is
`confirmed`.

## Timing: when is a sale final?

There is no single "finalized at" flag. In practice:

- A purchase's payment is final at `status === "confirmed"`.
- Fee and settlement data can be written **asynchronously after capture** (minutes to a
  couple of hours later), so if you need fees, don't treat a just-confirmed purchase as
  complete for fee purposes.
- Late corrections and refunds can arrive afterward.

A safe pattern is to only export purchases that have been settled for a few hours, and to
re-poll on the update timestamp to catch late changes.
